I am truly grateful and honored to be one of the students from VTU selected for the **Best Student Award** at a prestigious ceremony organized by **Lions International, Multiple District 317**, and **Negilayogi Samaja Seva Trust (R)**. The event took place at the esteemed **Senate Bhavan, Mysuru University** — a moment that will remain etched in my memory forever.

## **Award Ceremony Highlights**

#### **Distinguished Recognition**

I had the extraordinary privilege of receiving this prestigious award from **Parampoojya Jagadguru Dr. Nirmalanandanatha Swamiji**, the revered Peethadhipathi of Adi Chunchanagiri Mahasamsthana Math. His blessings made the occasion even more special and meaningful, adding spiritual significance to this academic achievement.

#### **Esteemed Dignitaries Present**

The ceremony was graced by distinguished dignitaries who honored the event with their presence, including **Dr. C.N. Ashwathnarayan**, Former Deputy CM and MLA from Malleshwaram, **Shri Yaduveer Krishnadatta Chamaraja Wadiyar**, Member of the Mysore Royal Family, and **Shri Adaguru H. Vishwanath**, Former Minister and MLC. Their presence elevated the significance of the ceremony and demonstrated the importance placed on recognizing academic excellence in our region.

## **Award Significance**

#### **What This Recognition Represents**

This prestigious award recognizes exceptional performance across multiple dimensions of academic and professional excellence. It acknowledges my outstanding performance in engineering studies with consistently high grades, deep expertise and innovation in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, demonstrated leadership in academic projects and community involvement, and active participation in cutting-edge research and innovative thinking. The recognition reflects not just academic achievement, but a holistic approach to engineering education that combines technical excellence with leadership and research contributions.

#### **The Selection Journey**

The path to this recognition involved multiple levels of rigorous evaluation. At the **university level**, I was chosen from among thousands of VTU students across Karnataka, demonstrating exceptional academic performance and leadership qualities. This achievement gained **state recognition** when I was acknowledged at the prestigious Mysuru University venue, highlighting the broader significance of the accomplishment. The award carries additional weight through **multi-organization endorsement**, being supported by both Lions International and educational trusts, which validates the comprehensive nature of the selection process and the high standards maintained.
