Overview

Recognized as one of the most distinguished students from VTU, I was honored with the Best Student Award at a prestigious ceremony organized by Lions International, Multiple District 317, and Negilayogi Samaja Seva Trust (R). This momentous recognition took place at the esteemed Senate Bhavan, Mysuru University, Karnataka’s most prestigious academic venue.
